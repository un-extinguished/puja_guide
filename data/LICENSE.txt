and the source rows in raw/ — is derived from OpenStreetMap, and is offered

Where the catalogue came from
-----------------------------
pujomap.com publishes its pandal rows under the ODbL at

    https://www.pujomap.com/data/pandals.json

stating in its own licence file that the rows it cannot sublicense are filtered
out of that copy before it is served. Those rows are therefore not in this
catalogue either. The rows that are published — names, zones, streets,
coordinates, and the nearest metro with its walking distance — were taken from
that copy and rebuilt here: normalised, de-duplicated, and extended with fields
this project derives (slugs, area groupings, station positions, walking routes).

What is derived, and how
------------------------
Every derived field is labelled as such in the output, so nobody has to guess
which numbers are surveyed and which are computed:

  stations[].lat / lon   Estimated. Each pandal publishes the walk to its
                         nearest station, never the station's own position.
                         The station is placed where the published walks fit
                         best, assuming a straight line of walk / 1.28.
                         `fitMeters` reports the typical error of that fit.
  routes[]               Generated from the coordinates: a seed pandal, the
                         stops nearest it, then a nearest-neighbour ordering.
                         `walkMeters` per leg is straight-line x 1.28.
  area / areaName        Derived from the published zone and latitude.
  neighbourhoodInferred  Set true where upstream is silent and the para was
                         taken from the nearest pandal that names one.
  position.*             Restates upstream's own geocodeMethod disclosure.

What is not here
----------------
Photographs. This build has none, and no page claims any. The upstream
catalogue credits photographs from Wikimedia Commons per card; that is their
work under its own licences and it is not copied here.

Using this data
---------------
Credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" wherever you show it. If you publish
this data, or anything derived from it, offer it under the ODbL too. Section
4.6 of the licence means that if you publish a map built from it you must also
offer the data — this project does that by serving these files alongside the
page, at /data/.
